# Goal Constitution and Humor Rubric: "Scroll-Stopping Satire"

## Strategy
Our core objective is to hijack the algorithm with unapologetic, fast-paced, satirical animated comedy built natively for Instagram Reels. We are not making gentle Pixar shorts; we are forging highly weaponized, 9:16 vertical brain-rot in the spirit of *Family Guy* and *South Park*. The goal is immediate retention, aggressive shareability, and high re-watch rates. We achieve this by targeting modern absurdities and internet culture through original, deeply flawed characters, delivering boundary-pushing punchlines that make the viewer laugh before they even realize they should be offended. 

## Style Decisions
Visually, the project relies on a deceptively simple, bright, and clean aesthetic—thick outlines, flat colors, and instantly recognizable character silhouettes. This sterile, friendly look serves as a trojan horse for the dark, edgy subject matter. Backgrounds should be minimalistic to keep focus on the character acting. Narrative escalation is our primary weapon: scenes should start mundane and spiral into absolute, unhinged chaos within 45 to 60 seconds (max 2 mins). Dialogue must be rapid-fire, devoid of awkward pauses unless explicitly used for deadpan comedic timing. 

## Conventions
1. **The 3-Second Hook:** Start *in media res*. No title cards, no fade-ins. The first frame must visually or audibly slap the viewer.
2. **Native 9:16 Framing:** Never crop a 16:9 video. Block the scene for the vertical axis—stack characters, use extreme close-ups, and utilize the top and bottom of the frame for sight gags.
3. **Aggressive Subtitling:** Subtitles are mandatory. They must be punchy (1-3 words per screen), boldly colored (yellow/white with heavy black strokes), and placed squarely in the safe zones, avoiding the UI overlays.
4. **Seamless Looping:** The final audio and visual frame must naturally bleed back into the hook to trick the viewer into a second loop.

## Exemplars
To understand the gold standard of edgy animated shorts, study the following creators:

1. **MeatCanyon** (http://instagram.com/meatcanyon)  
   *Why they are excellent:* A masterclass in grotesque escalation and unsettling pacing. They take recognizable tropes and twist them into visceral, horrific satire, using extreme close-ups and uncomfortable audio breathing to lock the viewer in.
2. **Flashgitz** (http://instagram.com/flashgitz)  
   *Why they are excellent:* Relentless, hyper-violent parodies that perfectly capture the *South Park* spirit of roasting everyone equally. Their animation is incredibly fluid, and they know exactly how to execute a high-impact, chaotic punchline. 
3. **Mashed** (http://instagram.com/mashed)  
   *Why they are excellent:* Though slightly broader in scope, their original shorts feature lightning-fast pacing and brilliant visual gags perfectly tailored to short-form attention spans, mixing dark humor with top-tier voice acting.

# Humor and Production Rubric

The numeric pass threshold is 8/10 for all deliverables. Anything scoring below this threshold in any category requires revision before rendering.

### Comedy
* **Score 4:** The joke is painfully predictable within the first 5 seconds. It relies entirely on hollow shock value (e.g., just swearing or gross-out humor) without a clever satirical punchline. Dialogue feels bloated and wastes the viewer's time.
* **Score 7:** Solid, coherent humor that hits the intended satirical marks. The pacing is good, and it earns a chuckle, but it plays it slightly too safe. It lacks that final, ruthless escalation that forces someone to immediately send the Reel to their group chat.
* **Score 9:** A masterclass in subversion. The satire bites hard without feeling preachy. The setup is tight, the punchlines are layered, and the escalation is completely unhinged but perfectly earned. Flawless comedic timing that demands multiple re-watches.

### Animation
* **Score 4:** Stiff, lifeless rigging that looks like a cheap flash cartoon from 2006. The 9:16 vertical space is poorly utilized, leaving awkward dead space at the top or bottom. Character acting adds absolutely nothing to the joke.
* **Score 7:** Competent and clean. Lip-sync is accurate, movements are relatively smooth, and the framing works fine for mobile. It includes a couple of decent visual gags but mostly relies on the audio to carry the weight.
* **Score 9:** Hyper-expressive and completely dynamic. The visual framing itself acts as a punchline. Uses custom smears, hilarious facial exaggerations, and background sight-gags that reward viewers who scrub through the video. Tailor-made for vertical screens.

### Audio
* **Score 4:** Muffled voice acting, peaking mics, or obvious room echo. Relies on overused, generic stock sound effects. The mixing is a mess, making the punchline hard to hear over the background noise.
* **Score 7:** Clear, professional dialogue and a balanced mix. Sound effects properly punctuate the physical comedy, and the background music sets the tone effectively without being distracting. 
* **Score 9:** Audio design that drives the humor as much as the visuals. Features immaculate, distinct voice acting. Employs layered, visceral sound design—using ear-assaulting bass boosts, perfectly timed silence, or wildly inappropriate music cues to add an entirely new dimension to the joke.
