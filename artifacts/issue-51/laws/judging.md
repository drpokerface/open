# judging - how a subjective score becomes a fact (pack: judging)
1. THE OUTSIDE ANCHOR: human quality is defined outside you. Before criteria.md, gather
   3-5 real exemplars of excellence in this exact medium (the internet when reachable; the
   constitution's anchors and library/calibration.md otherwise) and distill what is
   MEASURABLY true of the winners - structure, pacing, density, length, what the first
   seconds do, what all of them avoid. Those measurements become criteria; the exemplars
   become the judge's anchors, cited in criteria.md (source, what it exemplifies). A rubric
   invented from imagination is hollow. If nothing is reachable, record that in notes.md
   and anchor to the best thing you can actually inspect - never to nothing.
2. THE RUBRIC EXISTS BEFORE THE ARTIFACT, with anchored descriptors of what a 4, a 7, and
   a 9 concretely look like. Mechanical facts are binary; quality is a 0-10 score whose
   threshold comes from the goal text (default 8) plus a margin (0.5).
3. THE JUDGE is kit.judge(): a fresh call on the strongest attested model that sees ONLY
   the artifact, the rubric, and the anchors - never your reasoning, history, or excuses.
   Three samples, the median, each sample logged with its nearest anchor and a one-line
   justification. It also compares against the laziest acceptable version of the same
   deliverable: a pass must clearly beat that lazy baseline, not graze the threshold.
4. SELECTION beats wishing: for the creative core (premise, script, design, style),
   generate 3+ genuinely different cheap drafts, judge them blind, spend real budget only
   on the winner; refine while the median climbs; two flat rounds is a stall - climb the
   WHEN STUCK ladder.
5. Log median, margin, and turn in notes.md - a score without its evidence trail is a
   claim, not a fact.
