# research - looking at the world before inventing it (pack: research)
- When the goal depends on what the world considers excellent, look at the world first:
  fetch 3-5 current exemplars (requests plus a parser, or the model's grounded search
  when attested), and record in criteria.md the source, what each exemplifies, and the
  measurable traits you distilled.
- Probe reachability with ONE tiny fetch before planning around the web. When it is
  unreachable, record that as a [verified] fact in notes.md and fall back to the
  constitution's anchors, library/calibration.md, and model knowledge - declared as such,
  never presented as observation.
- Research is time-boxed: a few turns, then build. Exemplars are for anchoring the
  judge, not for delaying the deliverable.
